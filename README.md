## Librera Reader

*Reading*

Twelve formats, one reader. EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, DjVu, CBZ, CBR, TXT, RTF,
DOC and DOCX open in the same layout engine, so a book looks the same wherever it
came from. Margins, hyphenation, justification and font are yours to set once.

Google Play · Direct APK · Web app

## Screenshot Helper

*Utilities*

Capture, mark up, send. Crop the frame, blur what shouldn't be in it and share
without opening a gallery. The capture button sits in a floating bubble that stays
out of the shot.

Google Play · Direct APK

## Sound Icon

*Utilities*

Volume, one tap away. Media, ring, alarm and call sliders on a single panel that
opens from the status bar, with saved profiles for the places you switch between.

Google Play · Direct APK

## Nothing leaves your device.

Librera apps read the files already on your disk. No account is needed to open a
book or take a screenshot, no library is uploaded, and nothing is scanned for
advertising. Where an app asks for a permission, it asks at the moment it needs it
and works without it wherever that is possible.

App availability and pricing vary by region.
