## Librera Reader for Android

*Reading*

Highly customizable and feature-rich application for reading books in PDF, EPUB,
MOBI, DjVu, FB2, TXT, RTF, AZW, AZW3, HTML, CBZ, CBR formats on Android devices.
With its intuitive, yet powerful, interface, Librera makes ebook reading a
veritable pleasure. It even features a unique auto-scrolling, hands-free
Musician’s mode. As of today, it can boast more than 10 million downloads to
devices running all flavors of Android OS.

- [Google Play (Pro)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.foobnix.pro.pdf.reader)

## Librera1 Reader

*Reading*

A reader for the books you already have. Open an EPUB, PDF, FB2, MOBI or CBZ, and
read it on whichever device is to hand — the page you stopped on, the passages you
marked and the places you named follow you between them.

## Nothing leaves your device.

Librera apps read the files already on your disk. No account is needed to open a
book or take a screenshot, no library is uploaded, and nothing is scanned for
advertising. Where an app asks for a permission, it asks at the moment it needs it
and works without it wherever that is possible.

App availability and pricing vary by region.
