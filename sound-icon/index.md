## What problems does Sound Icon solve?

<ul class="features">
  <li class="feature"><h3>Multiple USB DACs</h3><p>“I have two USB DACs always connected: one for speakers and one for headphones. Sound Icon helps me switch the sound output in one mouse click.”</p></li>
  <li class="feature"><h3>Quick AirPods switch</h3><p>“I have AirPods connected to my MacBook and I want to instantly switch the output sound device to them.”</p></li>
  <li class="feature"><h3>Microphone input and levels</h3><p>“I need to easily switch the input microphone and adjust its level without going deep into the macOS System Settings.”</p></li>
</ul>

## Take control of your routing

Manage all of your input and output devices from one streamlined menu. Instantly see what is connected and where your sound is going without interrupting your workflow.

{% include gallery.html items=page.cards dir="/assets/img/apps/sound-icon/" %}

## Customize device icons

Make your setup visually yours. Assign unique Apple SF Symbols to every audio device you own, so you can recognize your headphones, external monitors or microphones — and your active output — at a glance.

## Easy switch on mouse click

Say goodbye to multi-step clicks. Star your favorite headphones and speakers, then right-click the menu bar icon to cycle through them without opening a menu — a fraction of a second.

{% include gallery.html items=page.mouse dir="/assets/img/apps/sound-icon/" kind="square" %}

## Built-in media controls

Play, pause, and skip tracks straight from your menu bar using native macOS media event integration. Middle-click the icon to play or pause whatever is playing.
